# Agard demo video script — target under 3 minutes (aim 2:30)

Record ONLY from the final build. If sandbox is live, show the sandbox
approve link. If sandbox is not yet live, do NOT record a final video
claiming a real checkout — record the mock flow and label it MOCK on screen.
This script is ready to record; the final take must match the build shown.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem (20s)
On screen: an AI agent shopping on its own.
Narration: "AI agents can shop for you now. But who stops an agent buying
the wrong thing, from a new seller, for six times your budget? Agard
is the guardrail in front of the cart."

## 0:20–0:50 — Ask the agent (30s)
On screen: type "earbuds", budget $100, click Ask the agent.
Narration: "I ask for earbuds under a hundred dollars. Agard ranks
the options and scores every one before any money moves — price against
my usual spend, merchant history, and my budget."

## 0:50–1:30 — The risky one is blocked (40s)
On screen: $299 Wireless Earbuds Ultra, new merchant, risk 100, BLOCKED.
Narration: "This one is two hundred ninety-nine dollars from a seller
I've never used, over my budget. Risk one hundred. Agard blocks it.
There is no approve button. Nothing can be charged by accident."
Caption on screen: BLOCKED before checkout.

## 1:30–2:10 — The safe one is approved (40s)
On screen: $49.99 Wireless Earbuds Pro, established merchant, risk 0.
Click Approve checkout.
Narration: "This one fits: forty-nine ninety-nine, a merchant I know,
inside budget, risk zero. I approve it — and only now does checkout
start." [Sandbox take: show the PayPal sandbox order + approve link.
Mock take: show MOCK_APPROVED label clearly on screen.]

## 2:10–2:30 — What judges can check (20s)
On screen: Transactions list, then repo + README.
Narration: "The safe purchase is recorded. The blocked one never is.
Code, run instructions, and honest limits are in the public repo.
Agard: the agent that guards every buy."

## On-screen honesty footer (all takes)
"Sandbox / synthetic data only. No real money. Guard is a statistical
